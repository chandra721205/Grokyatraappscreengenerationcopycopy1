# Educational Tourism - Quick Access Guide

## 🎓 How to Use the New Educational Tourism Feature

### Step 1: Launch the App
Start the GrokYatra app and complete onboarding (or navigate to the main home if already onboarded).

### Step 2: Find Educational Category
On the main home screen, scroll down to **"Browse Categories"** section. Look for the **Educational** card with a blue graduation cap icon 🎓.

### Step 3: Explore Sub-Categories
Click the Educational card to see 4 educational sub-categories:
- 🎒 School Groups (K-12)
- 🎓 College & University Groups
- 🔬 Research Groups & Academic Delegations
- 📚 Individual Researchers & Students

### Step 4: Browse Programs
Each sub-category shows admin-managed placeholder programs. All content is labeled with:
- Gray backgrounds on placeholder text
- "[Admin: ...]" notation
- Purple banner explaining admin-driven content

### Step 5: Discover Real Institutions
Use the **Google Search** or **YouTube Browse** buttons to find real educational institutions and programs.

### Step 6: Express Interest
Click **"Save Interest"** on any program to fill out a comprehensive form:
- Group details (size, participant type, age range)
- Travel preferences (dates, duration)
- Academic focus (field of study, learning mode, pace)
- Budget range (with slider)
- Notification preferences (WhatsApp/Email/SMS)

### Step 7: Submit & Get Notified
After submitting, you'll receive a confirmation message. The admin will be notified of your interest and can match you with suitable programs.

- Comprehensive form with 12+ fields
- Save preferences for future matching
- Get notified when admin publishes matching programs

### Discovery Tools
- **Google Search**: Opens Google with relevant educational queries
- **YouTube Browse**: Opens YouTube to watch institution videos
- Available on every screen

### Visual Indicators
- **Purple banner**: Admin-driven content notice
- **Gray backgrounds**: Placeholder text
- **Bracket notation**: [Admin: Update This Text]

5. **Browse Multiple Categories**: Compare different sub-categories before expressing interest

## 🎯 What Makes This Different

Unlike other tourism categories that show specific destinations, Educational Tourism:
- Shows **admin placeholders only** (no real institution names)
- Encourages **external discovery** (Google/YouTube)
- Focuses on **interest capture** (not booking)
- Provides **comprehensive forms** for detailed preferences
- Integrates **Grok AI** for admin assistance
