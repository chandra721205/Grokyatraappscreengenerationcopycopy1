## 🎯 The 4 Pathways

### 1. 🎒 School Groups (K-12)
**Subtitle:** Field trips, safety-first history & science tours  
**Type:** Group Booking  
**Form:** Plan Institutional Trip

**Key Fields:**
- Curriculum Focus (History/Science/Nature/Industry)
- Group Size (Students + Faculty)
- ✓ Safety: Teacher Ratio, Doctor, Lady Attendant
- ✓ Concessions: Railway, CSR Grant

---

### 2. 🎓 College & University Groups
**Subtitle:** Industrial visits, career insights, technical workshops  
**Type:** Group Booking  
**Form:** Plan Institutional Trip

**Key Fields:**
- Same as School Groups
- Focus on industry/technical curriculum
- Authorization letter required

---

### 3. 🔬 Research Groups & Academic Delegations
**Subtitle:** PhD scholars, lab visits, archive access  
**Type:** Research Access  
**Form:** Request Research Access

**Key Fields:**
- Focus Area (Archives/Lab/Field Study)
- Institution Details
- ✓ ASI/Govt Site Permissions

---

### 4. 📚 Individual Researchers & Students
**Subtitle:** Flexible independent study & field research  
**Type:** Research Access  
**Form:** Request Research Access

**Key Fields:**
- Same as Research Groups
- Individual or small team focus

## 🎯 Best Practices

### For Schools:
1. Have authorization letter ready
2. Know exact student/faculty count
3. Specify safety requirements upfront
4. Apply for concessions early

### For Researchers:
1. Provide institution credentials
2. Specify permit needs clearly
3. Allow 1-3 months for approvals
4. Detail research focus area
