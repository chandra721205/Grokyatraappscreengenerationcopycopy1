## 🎨 DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### Typography
- **Headers**: 3xl-4xl, bold, white on gradients
- **Subheaders**: xl-2xl, bold, gray-900
- **Body**: sm-base, regular, gray-600/700
- **Labels**: xs, semibold, gray-800
- **Admin Placeholders**: Brackets format `[Admin: ...]`

### Spacing
- **Screen Padding**: 24px (px-6)
- **Section Margins**: 24px (mb-6)
- **Card Padding**: 24px (p-6)
- **Element Gaps**: 12-16px

### Borders & Shadows
- **Rounded Corners**: 24px-32px (rounded-3xl, rounded-[2rem])
- **Shadows**: xl-2xl for cards
- **Borders**: 2px solid, matching theme colors

### Icons
- **Source**: Lucide React
- **Sizes**: 
  - Small: 16px (w-4 h-4)
  - Medium: 20px (w-5 h-5)
  - Large: 24-28px (w-6 h-6, w-7 h-7)
  - Extra Large: 44px (w-11 h-11)

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Gradient backgrounds, h-14, rounded-2xl
- **Secondary**: Outlined, white backgrounds
- **Icon Buttons**: Square, rounded-xl, 44×44 or 36×36

### Cards
- **Background**: White or gradient (featured)
- **Padding**: 20-24px
- **Rounded**: 24-32px
- **Border**: 2px when not featured
- **Hover**: Scale 1.02, shadow increase
